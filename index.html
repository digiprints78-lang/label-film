FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Clear 3D Heat Transfer Label Film used for?

It is designed for transparent, raised garment labels, custom clothing logos and decorative heat-transfer designs. The transparent base allows the fabric color beneath the label to remain visible.

What makes Clear Label Film different from White Label Film?

Clear Label Film has a transparent base for cutout designs and natural fabric integration. White Label Film has an opaque white base that provides stronger visual contrast for colorful graphics.

What printer and consumables are required?

A compatible UV flatbed printer is recommended, together with UV 3D Label Ink (CMYKW) and UV 3D Label Ink GL (Varnish). A suitable cutter and heat press are also required.

Can I use Clear Label Film on different fabrics?

The film is developed for garment decoration. Actual compatibility depends on fabric composition, surface structure and heat sensitivity. Testing is recommended before production.

Does Clear Label Film support custom designs and short runs?

Yes. UV digital printing lets you update artwork digitally and produce personalized designs without preparing a new mold for every design change.

What are the recommended heat-transfer settings?

The product specification lists 150–160°C for 15–20 seconds at 3 kg pressure. Please verify the final settings with a test on your own fabric and equipment.
